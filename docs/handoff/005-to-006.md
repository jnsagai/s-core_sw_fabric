## Boundary for 006

Fabro is the only workflow execution and run-state platform. Increment 006 may integrate an
external run with the 005 evidence intake contract by submitting complete raw output bytes,
exact input/process/tool/policy/profile/subject bindings, and a receipt from an owner-controlled
collector. Fabro success, interview answers, replay text, and claimed actors are not signed
measurements or human decisions. The 005 CLI has no signing, approval, or execution command.

A production pass remains blocked until owners provision and review an independent production
trust root, authenticated collector, human decision submission, role and independence registry,
revocation service, reviewed gate policy, and protected time basis. A test key or fixture
`fixture_contract` profile cannot be promoted to `production`. Increment 006 must retain the
version-1 domain separation and use immutable references when registering or resuming runs.
Module/platform readiness and release gates belong to 014 and later increments.

The fixture-domain mutation and limit matrices and requirement reconciliation are recorded in
[005 acceptance](../../specs/005-trusted-evidence-and-human-gates/acceptance.md). T009 remains open
in [005 tasks](../../specs/005-trusted-evidence-and-human-gates/tasks.md): an independently
protected production trust-root pin and its owner review are not available. The fixture-domain
implementation cannot be promoted by copying its test key or selecting a request time.
