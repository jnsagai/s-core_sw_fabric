Increment 004 provides deterministic native artifact indexes, sealed candidate overlays,
expected-set trace coverage, conservative impact, categorized semantic diff, and direct-drift
detection. Native RST and the pinned S-CORE metamodel remain authoritative. All persisted reports
retain registration, execution, evidence, acceptance, engineering readiness, and release as
`not_evaluated`.

## Available contracts

- Eight strict version-1 schemas under `schemas/`.
- Pending-production artifact/build/trace profiles with explicit reviewed fixture overrides.
- Exact module-template, Bazel, dependency-lock, metamodel, source-mount, and hash-bound template
  identities.
- `score-fabric artifact index|candidate|validate|trace|diff|drift` with stable 0/1/2 exits.
- Complete snapshot and candidate closure, exact digests, source maps, fresh native receipts,
  expected obligations, coverage sets, impact paths, and portable reports.
- Licensed feature/component/analysis fixtures, a blocked missing-verification case, and a
  non-skipped locked Bazel feature/component/analysis index/create/update journey.
- Inclusive finite limits for controls, packages, targets, files, text, entities, obligations,
  relations, edits, findings, native output, and native command time.

The final 004 identities and evidence are recorded in
[the acceptance record](../../specs/004-native-artifact-traceability/acceptance.md). The exact
source/profile/package bindings must be carried into 005 rather than reconstructed from titles,
paths, or generated output.

## Retained boundaries and pending inputs

- Production identifier allocation, artifact mapping, trace policy, and target-owner review remain
  pending. Fixture review and real native build compatibility do not grant production authority.
- Increment 005 may bind trusted evidence and authenticated human decisions only to exact 004
  candidate, report, source, tool, and profile identities.
- Structural validity, native build success, trace existence, coverage, impact, and clean drift do
  not establish evidence trust, adequacy, approval, readiness, release, or deployment.
- Historical candidate/report facts remain immutable; changed sources, profiles, templates,
  metamodels, validator identities, or evidence require explicit staleness and re-evaluation.
- Fabro remains the sole future workflow runtime. Increment 005 must define evidence and human-gate
  contracts without implementing FAB-019-FAB-023 runtime registration or execution behavior.
